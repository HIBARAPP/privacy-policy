Common Troubleshooting

  • Connection Issues: Ensure your Long-Lived Access Token is correct and your Mac can reach your Home Assistant URL.
  • Demo Mode: You can test all features without a server by toggling "Demo Mode" in the app settings.
  • Restoring Purchases: If you reinstalled the app, use the "Restore Purchases" button on the upgrade screen to reactivate your Lifetime access.
